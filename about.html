About The Artist

Creating meaningful artwork through passion, imagination and attention to detail.

My name is Raghd, the artist behind Raghd Art. Drawing has always been more than a hobby—it is my way of expressing emotions, preserving memories, and telling stories through every brushstroke.

I specialize in custom portraits, realistic drawings, oil paintings and digital artwork. Every commission is carefully created to reflect each client's vision while maintaining high artistic quality and attention to detail.

My goal is to create timeless artwork that becomes a meaningful part of people's lives and homes.

My Artistic Journey

Every artist has a beginning. Mine started with simple sketches and grew into a passion for creating meaningful artwork that tells stories and captures emotions.

2015

First Sketches

My artistic journey began with simple pencil drawings, exploring shapes, faces and everyday objects while discovering my passion for art.

2017

Building Strong Foundations

Learning perspective, proportions and realistic shading became the foundation of my artistic growth.

2019

Discovering My Style

I experimented with portraits, realism and different artistic techniques to develop my own visual identity.

2021

Sharing My Artwork

I began sharing my work online, receiving valuable feedback that inspired continuous improvement.

2023

Professional Development

My focus shifted toward creating high-quality custom commissions while refining both traditional and digital art techniques.

2025

Launching Raghd Art

I created my personal brand, Raghd Art, offering personalized commissions and building my online portfolio.

2026

Growing Beyond Limits

Today, Raghd Art continues to grow with the vision of creating meaningful artwork for clients worldwide while constantly learning, improving and exploring new creative possibilities.
